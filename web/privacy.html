Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

MyAnimeDex ("we", "us") is an app for tracking anime. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the MyAnimeDex app and website, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, your username and, if you add one, your profile photo. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address they share with us (Apple may give us a private relay address).
  • Your content: your anime list, statuses, episode progress, favorites, ratings, reviews, custom lists, tags and notes, Arena results, poll votes and the recommendation feedback you give. If you are a Premium member, copies of your list are kept as backups.
  • Usage data: which screens and features you use, the app version, device model, operating system and language. This is linked to a random identifier and, after you sign in, to your account ID. It never includes your email address or the text you write.
  • Advertising data: when ads are shown, Google AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (used for approximate location) and how you interact with ads.
  • Purchase data: if you subscribe to Premium, we receive your subscription status and purchase history from the App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.

You can browse the app without an account. Guests are only covered by the usage and advertising data above.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to create and run your account, save your list and sync it across your devices, show your profile and reviews to other users, send the episode reminders you turn on, provide Premium features and backups, understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and improve it, and show ads in the free version. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Services we work with

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • AniList: anime information. If you import your list from AniList, your public AniList username is sent to AniList.
  • Google AdMob: ads.
  • RevenueCat: Premium subscriptions.
  • PostHog (servers in the EU): product analytics in the app, and anonymous page view statistics on this website. The website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and anonymizes IP addresses.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: signing in.
  • Google Translate: translating anime synopses. Only the synopsis text is sent.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

4. Ads and tracking

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. On iPhone and iPad we ask for your permission before your advertising identifier is used; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Premium members do not see ads.

5. Permissions on your device

  • Notifications: episode and trial reminders are scheduled on your device and are only sent if you allow notifications.
  • Calendar: only when you tap "Add to calendar" do we add airing times to your phone's calendar. We do not read or upload your calendar events.
  • Camera and photos: only used to take or choose a profile photo.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings.

6. What other users can see

Your username, profile photo, level, profile showcase (such as your Top 10 and stats card, if you turn it on), reviews, the lists you share and your watch activity in Community Pulse are visible to other users. Your email address, notes, tags and backups are private.

7. Security, retention and deletion

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and is protected by access rules so that only you can change it. We keep your data for as long as your account exists, and only the last 7 backups are kept. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile > Danger Zone > Delete Account) or as explained at myanimedex.com/delete-account. This permanently deletes your account, list, reviews, backups and other content from our database. To also remove your profile photo file or your analytics data, email us and we will delete them.

8. Children

MyAnimeDex is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we process it. Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the EU and the United States.

10. Contact and changes

For questions or requests, email support@myanimedex.com. When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
