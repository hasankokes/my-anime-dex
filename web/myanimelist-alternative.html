A MyAnimeList alternative for your phone: move your list to MyAnimeDex

Updated November 3, 2026

MyAnimeDex is a free anime tracker for iPhone, iPad and Android that imports your MyAnimeList list in about a minute, keeping statuses, episodes watched, dates and scores. On top of your list it adds a weekly airing calendar in your own time zone, reminders when new episodes air and ranked lists you can share.

How do I move my MyAnimeList list to MyAnimeDex?

  1. On MyAnimeList, open Export, choose Anime List and download the file. It is a .xml.gz file; you don't need to unzip it.
  2. Install MyAnimeDex from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with Apple, Google or email.
  3. Go to Profile > Data & Backup > Import MyAnimeList file and pick the file.
  4. Choose whether to overwrite anime already in your list (off by default: those are skipped) and wait while each anime is matched. A summary shows how many were added, updated and skipped.

Coming from AniList instead? Type your AniList username in the same screen. The list has to be public on AniList.

What comes along with the import?

StatusesWatching, Completed, On Hold, Dropped and Plan to Watch
ProgressEpisodes watched for every anime
ScoresYour 1–10 scores, converted to the app's 5-star rating (a 9 or 10 becomes 5 stars)
DatesStart and finish dates
Your MyAnimeList accountNot changed. The import is one-way.

What does MyAnimeDex add on top of your list?

  • One-tap episode tracking. Tap + after an episode; a show moves to Completed on its own after the last one.
  • Airing calendar in your time zone. A weekly schedule with live countdowns, filterable to your favorites.
  • Episode reminders. Free notifications when a favorite anime airs.
  • Ranked lists. Drag to reorder, add a score and a one-line take, share a link or build a list together with friends.
  • XP and ranks. Earn XP for what you watch, review and rank, climb from Rookie to Kami and check the leaderboard.
  • Anime Arena. Bracket battles in 20 categories to crown your favorite.
  • Guest mode and 15 languages. Browse without an account; the app speaks English, Turkish, Japanese, Russian, German, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Hindi, French, Italian, Polish, Vietnamese and Thai.

The free version shows ads. Optional Premium adds My Stats (genre and studio breakdowns), themes and profile cosmetics, For You+ recommendations, tags and notes, My Week, adding episodes to your phone calendar, cloud backup and export, and removes ads.

Is MyAnimeDex affiliated with MyAnimeList?

No. MyAnimeDex is an independent app; its anime titles, episodes, airing times and scores come from AniList. MyAnimeList is a trademark of its owner and is named here only to describe the import.
