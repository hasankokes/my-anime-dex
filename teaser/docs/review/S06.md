### Render note (all three shots)
Video renders were **non-deterministic** at 1080p with default concurrency: random breakout layers (rows, PLATINUM line, stat chips, die faces, once even the phone video) were missing in single frames, different on every run, while `remotion still` was always correct. Measured by rendering twice and diffing per-frame luma: it disappears at `--scale=0.5` and at `--concurrency=1`, so it is GPU/compositor memory pressure in the installed Chrome (remotion.config.ts uses it because chrome-headless-shell crashes). Mitigations in these shots: Card3D (one transformed element per layer, own perspective root) instead of shared preserve-3d Layer3D for flat UI; box-shadow instead of drop-shadow filters; SoftGlow (no blur filter); GlossyDie without per-face brightness filters. After that S04-L was stable at default concurrency, S05-L had 1 bad frame, S06 still flaked → **final review videos rendered with `--concurrency=1` (verified identical across two runs).**
